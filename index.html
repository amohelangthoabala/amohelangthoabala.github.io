The work

VENTIQ.
Two products, one platform.

I founded VENTIQ and built it end to end: product, design, code and operations. Boards, NGOs and event organisers in Lesotho run on it.

ventiq.co.ls/sessions
AI minutes
VENTIQ Sessions

Meetings that write their own minutes

Record on a phone, follow on a laptop, and have formal minutes moments after the meeting ends.

  • Live capture across devices, kept in step in real time
  • Whisper transcription and local LLMs: meeting audio never leaves the country
  • QR self check-in, signed registers, certificates and action points
  • Ask questions across a whole meeting history (embeddings and RAG)
VENTIQ Events

Ticketing, payments and the door

For organisers selling tickets in Lesotho, from the listing to admission.

  • EcoCash and M-Pesa payments with verification and reconciliation
  • QR tickets and a React Native scanner app for admission
  • WhatsApp and email notifications
  • Fees invoiced automatically into the accounting system
How it's built

A small budget,
run like a serious system.

Tested, secured and measured, with private AI on our own hardware.

Product engineering

Laravel 12, Inertia, React and TypeScript, Tailwind and shadcn/ui. Multi-tenant isolation, role-based access, usage billing, 500+ Pest tests.

Local, private AI

Whisper and Ollama on dedicated hardware in Lesotho, over a private network. Long meetings summarised in parts so nothing is lost.

Integrations

Mobile money through PayLesotho, the WhatsApp Cloud API, transactional email and accounting APIs for invoices.

Operations

Ubuntu, nginx and PHP-FPM; deploys that roll back on failure; hardened servers, security audits, load tests and a capacity dashboard.
