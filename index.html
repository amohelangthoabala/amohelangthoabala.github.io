The work

VENTIQ: two products, one platform

I founded VENTIQ and built it end to end: product, design, code and operations. Organisations in Lesotho use it to sell tickets and run admission, and to run meetings that end with minutes, a signed attendance register and action points, written by AI that never leaves the country.

VENTIQ Sessions

Meetings that write their own minutes

Record a meeting on a phone, follow it on a laptop, and get formal minutes minutes after it ends.

  • Live capture across laptop and phone, kept in step in real time; any screen can move the agenda and the recording follows.
  • Speech transcribed with Whisper and written up by locally hosted LLMs; long discussions handled in parts so nothing is lost.
  • Self check-in by QR code with digital signatures, attendance registers, certificates and action points that carry over.
  • Ask questions across an organisation's meeting history (embeddings and RAG); the next meeting opens with the last one's minutes, corrections recorded.
From meeting to record

Minutes, registers and follow-up

What boards, committees and NGOs need on file, without anyone typing it up afterwards.

  • Formal minutes by agenda item, reviewed and downloaded as PDF, with the attendance register attached.
  • Action points with owners and due dates, and reminders by WhatsApp or email.
  • Plans metered by meeting hours, pilots, and VENTIQ staff who can run sessions for clients.
VENTIQ Events

Ticketing, payments and admission

For organisers selling tickets in Lesotho, from the listing to the door.

  • Registration and ticket sales with EcoCash and M-Pesa through PayLesotho, with verification and reconciliation.
  • QR tickets, and a React Native scanner app for admission at the door.
  • WhatsApp and email notifications, and fees invoiced automatically into the accounting system.
How it's built

Engineering behind it

A small team's budget, run like a serious system: tested, secured, measured.

Product engineering

Laravel 12 with Inertia.js, React and TypeScript, Tailwind and shadcn/ui; migrated from Blade, Alpine and Filament. Multi-tenant data isolation, role-based access, usage-based billing, 500+ tests in Pest.

Local, private AI

Whisper and Ollama on dedicated hardware in Lesotho, reached over a private network (Tailscale): meeting audio is transcribed and summarised in the country, not by a foreign AI service.

Integrations

Mobile money (EcoCash, M-Pesa via PayLesotho), the WhatsApp Cloud API for notifications and certificates, transactional email, and accounting APIs for invoices.

Operations

Ubuntu, nginx and PHP-FPM; automated deployment that rolls back on failure; systemd workers, timers and cron; hardened nginx and PHP, automated security audits, CI builds; load testing and a capacity dashboard to plan growth.
